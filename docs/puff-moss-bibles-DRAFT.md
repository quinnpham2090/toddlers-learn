## Puff — the fluffy cloud sprite

- **Silhouette**: soft cloud-shaped fluffy body made of overlapping rounded
  cloud puffs; fluffy cloud arms that often wave hello. Round and soft, reads
  as a cloud at thumbnail size.
- **Palette (exact)**: body soft white `#FBFCFE`; shading light sky-blue
  `#A9D3F0`; eyes brown `#6B4A2F`; cheeks pink `#F2A7B4`.
- **Outfit (exact, same every image)**: striped sky-blue scarf `#7FB5E0` over
  `#FFFFFF` — this IS the outfit; no other clothing.
- **Props**: small basket of flowers (proven at RunningHub) — the only prop.
- **Face**: round friendly face, big brown eyes, pink cheeks, gentle happy
  expression.
- **Personality (child-directed, in the guides' voice)**:
  - "Hello, friend! Shall we wave hello together?"
  - "Look what is in my flower basket — let us see together!"
- **Hard negatives**: never a human child, never a person; no hair; no
  necklace; no human ears; no other clothing beyond the scarf; no legs/feet
  (cloud body only).

## Moss — the round mushroom-cap sprite

- **Silhouette**: round mushroom-cap sprite — a terracotta-brown mushroom cap
  with cream spots on top of a cream round face and body; reads as a round
  mushroom at thumbnail size.
- **Palette (exact)**: cap terracotta-brown `#B5651D`; spots, face and body
  cream `#F7EBD6`; eyes brown `#6B4A2F`; cheeks pink `#F2A7B4`; vest
  sage-green `#9CAF88`; shorts brown `#6B4A2F`.
- **Outfit (exact, same every image)**: little sage-green vest over brown
  shorts — fully dressed; no other clothing.
- **Props**: small potted sprout and small silver `#C0C4C8` watering can
  (both proven at RunningHub).
- **Face**: cream round face, big brown eyes, pink cheeks, warm friendly smile.
- **Personality (child-directed, in the guides' voice)**:
  - "What does our little sprout need today? Let us look together."
  - "We can water gently and take our time."
- **Hard negatives**: never a human child, never a person; no hair; no
  necklace; no human ears (the cap is the head); no bare torso (always
  vested); no other props.

## Originality notes (DRAFT — pattern screen only, not legal clearance)

### Puff

Nearest lookalikes: the Stay Puft Marshmallow Man (round white cloud-like
figure), Kirby (round soft-bodied character), generic weather/cloud app
mascots.

Distinguishing features (at least three):

1. **Silhouette** — overlapping cloud-puff body with fluffy cloud arms, not a
   smooth rounded marshmallow/ball body.
2. **Palette/shading** — light sky-blue shading inside a soft-white cloud body;
   Stay Puft is plain white, Kirby is solid pink.
3. **Outfit** — a striped sky-blue scarf is the sole outfit item; the lookalikes
   have no scarf (or a full costume).
4. **Props** — a small basket of flowers, unique to this character.

### Moss

Nearest lookalikes: Toad (Nintendo mushroom character), generic mushroom
mascots / Mushroom-Kingdom-style sprites, Smurf-like capped sprites.

Distinguishing features (at least three):

1. **Cap** — terracotta-brown cap with cream spots (Toad's cap is red/white or
   white/red); the terracotta-and-cream palette is the anchor difference.
2. **Body/proportions** — a single round cream body where cap meets face, not a
   humanoid mushroom with separate head/body and small stature.
3. **Outfit** — sage-green vest over brown shorts (Toad wears a blue vest);
   fully dressed, no bare arms or legs.
4. **Props** — a small potted sprout plus a silver watering can, neither of
   which the lookalikes carry.
